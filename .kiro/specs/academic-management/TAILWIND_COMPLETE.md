### 🚀 Key Improvements

**Before (Bootstrap):**
```html
<div class="card">
    <div class="card-header">
        <h3 class="card-title">Title</h3>
    </div>
    <div class="card-body">
        Content
    </div>
</div>
```

**After (Tailwind):**
```html
<div class="bg-white rounded-lg shadow-lg">
    <div class="px-6 py-4 border-b border-gray-200">
        <h3 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900">Title</h3>
    </div>
    <div class="p-6">
        Content
    </div>
</div>
```
